Delete your Blurtle account

Blurtle is a video effects app by Starry Works.

In the app

  1. Open Blurtle.
  2. Tap your picture at the top right of Home.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete account again to confirm.

Without the app

Email tahalakdawala03@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Blurtle account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

Your account (name, email address, account ID), your usage counts, save stats, the error reports and feature ideas linked to your account. Nothing is kept afterwards. Videos and edits on your phone are never on our servers, so they stay on your phone until you remove the app.

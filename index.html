Blurtle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Blurtle is a video effects app by Starry Works. It processes your videos entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Blurtle does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Your videos, photos and exports never leave your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Permissions

Blurtle only accesses the video files you choose to import and saves the finished videos you export to your device.

Children

Blurtle is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Blurtle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Blurtle is a video effects app by Starry Works. It processes your videos entirely on your device.

What we collect

Your videos, photos and exports never leave your device. Blurtle has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Error reports

When something goes wrong in the app (a crash, a save that fails, or a video that won't open), Blurtle sends an anonymous error report so we can fix the bug. A report contains:

  • the error message and where in the app it happened
  • the app version, Android version and phone model
  • basic settings of the edit being saved (such as length, size and frame rate)

A report never contains your videos, photos, file names, account details, location or any identifier that links it to you or your device. File paths are removed before sending. If your phone is offline, the report waits on the device and is sent later.

Reports are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and are only used to find and fix bugs. They are not shared with anyone else, not sold and not used for ads. Reports are kept only as long as they are needed to fix bugs.

Permissions

Blurtle only accesses the video files you choose to import and saves the finished videos you export to your device.

Children

Blurtle is not directed at children under 13. The only data it sends are the anonymous error reports described above, which contain no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Blurtle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Blurtle is a video effects app by Starry Works. It processes your videos entirely on your device.

What we collect

Your videos, photos and exports never leave your device. Blurtle has no ads and does no tracking across other apps or websites.

Your account

When you start Blurtle you can sign in with Google or skip. If you sign in, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you skip, Blurtle gives you an anonymous account ID instead, with no name or email; signing in later links it to your Google account. Either way we keep:

  • an account ID, and your name and email address if you signed in
  • when you first and last opened the app, how many times you opened it, and how many edits and saved videos you have
  • the app version, Android version, phone model, screen size, app language, region and time zone

We use this to keep your account, to understand how Blurtle is used on different phones so we can improve it, to match error reports and ideas to the person who sent them, and to reply to you. We never post anything to your Google account and never see your Google password.

Save stats

When a video finishes saving, Blurtle records how it was made: its length, the number of clips, frame rate, resolution, shape, which effects were used (for example "velocity, blur, flash") and how long the save took. It never contains the video, its frames, file names or text you typed on the video. We use it to see which features people use and how fast saves are, so we can make them faster.

Error reports

When something goes wrong in the app (a crash, a save that fails, or a video that won't open), Blurtle sends an error report so we can fix the bug. A report contains:

  • the error message and where in the app it happened
  • the app version, Android version and phone model
  • basic settings of the edit being saved (such as length, size and frame rate)
  • your account ID, if you are signed in

A report never contains your videos, photos, file names or location. File paths are removed before sending. If your phone is offline, the report waits on the device and is sent later.

Reports are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and are only used to find and fix bugs. They are not shared with anyone else, not sold and not used for ads. Reports are kept only as long as they are needed to fix bugs.

Feature ideas

If you choose to send an idea from "Suggest a feature", Blurtle sends:

  • the topics you picked and the note you typed, if any
  • where you sent it from (Home or after a save) and how many videos you have saved
  • the app language, app version, Android version and phone model
  • your account ID, so we can reply to you

Ideas are only sent when you tap send. They contain no videos, photos, file names or location; please don't type personal details in the note. They are stored with Supabase like error reports, only used to decide what to build next, never shared, sold or used for ads.

Where it is kept

Your account, save stats, error reports and ideas are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and sent over an encrypted connection. They are not shared with anyone else, not sold and not used for ads. We keep them while your account exists.

Deleting your account

In Blurtle, tap your picture at the top of Home, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything linked to it (save stats, error reports and ideas) from our servers right away. Edits on your phone stay on your phone. If you skipped signing in, or no longer have the app, see how to ask us to delete your account.

Permissions

Blurtle only accesses the video files you choose to import and saves the finished videos you export to your device.

Children

Blurtle is not directed at children under 13, and signing in needs a Google account, which Google only gives on its own to people old enough under their country's rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
